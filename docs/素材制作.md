# 素材制作

## 应用图标（2026-10-15，首个公开内测版）

使用内置imagegen，以项目现有`assets/rin/poses.png`作为角色与画风参考，生成一幅新的Rin、平板与小白花图标。图稿保留粉棕长发、白色蝴蝶结、浅色衣服与安静微笑，浅蓝圆角底牌之外为真实透明通道。

成品为`assets/app/rin-shelter.png`（1254×1254 RGBA原稿）和`assets/app/rin-shelter.ico`（16/24/32/48/64/128/256像素）。原始生成PNG原样复制入项目；ICO仅做标准格式转换与尺寸重采样，不重新绘制。转换脚本为`scripts/convert-app-icon.py`，运行时和正常.NET构建使用已交付ICO，不需要Python。

同一ICO用于EXE的Windows图标和WPF内嵌资源；陪伴窗口、设置窗口与托盘统一读取。托盘的独立Icon克隆随窗口退出释放，不改变角色/场景素材、alpha命中或行为。该资源没有场景落点或肢体锚点，不纳入角色Atlas。原稿和ICO随源码与便携包交付。

### 实际imagegen提示词

```text
Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: production Windows application icon for Rin's Little Shelter, one square icon, not a mockup or sprite sheet. Input image 1 is ONLY the character identity and soft anime illustration reference; do not copy its grid or multiple poses. Create ONE clean, polished head-and-shoulders illustration of the same older Rin, with muted pink-auburn long hair, her distinctive large white hair bow, calm brown eyes and a gentle closed-mouth smile, pale cream-white short-sleeved blouse. She holds a simple small cyan tablet at her chest, and a tiny white daisy with a warm yellow center and two sage-green leaves grows beside the tablet, expressing quietly creating a little world. Compose a compact, easily recognizable silhouette, large face and hair, simple broad color areas and a few crisp contours that still read at 16/32/48 pixels. Preserve the project's gentle half-chibi anime style; do not make her a baby or over-cute. Center her within a soft sky-blue rounded-square badge with a warm white highlight, leaving 5 percent transparent padding outside the badge. The outer background must have real transparent alpha, not a checkerboard painted in the image. Gentle clean daylight, muted rosy hair, warm cream, soft cyan and small sage accents; enough contrast for a Windows taskbar and tray on light and dark backgrounds. No typography, letters, words, captions, logos, watermark, device mockup, multiple icons, scenery, detailed background, neon, bloom, or excessive decorative particles. One finished square 1024x1024 app icon; keep all hair and bow fully inside the badge.
```

提示词请求1024方图，内置工具实际返回1254方图；原稿尺寸如实保留，Windows ICO按上述标准尺寸交付。没有使用CLI或外部API密钥。

技术接入参考微软的[Window.Icon](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dotnet/api/system.windows.window.icon?view=windowsdesktop-10.0)与[WPF资源读取](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dotnet/api/system.windows.application.getresourcestream?view=windowsdesktop-10.0)说明。最终实际检查与未测项目登记于[验证记录](验证记录.md)。

2026-10-12 多场景扩展新增的场景、秋千及幼年Rin/父亲素材，见[多场景素材制作](多场景素材制作.md)。下文为原有基础角色与屋外素材的制作记录；基础姿势只使用成年Rin，记忆另有独立幼年素材。

全部交付的角色与世界素材由内置 imagegen 工具生成，使用真正透明背景；未使用CLI或外部API密钥。最终素材包括 `assets/rin/poses.png`、`walk.png`、`trot-v2.png`、`hop.png`、`turn.png` 与 `assets/world/objects.png`。程序按资源清单读取图集，再进行透明边距裁切与显示缩放。

角色参考来自 [Shelter 官网](https://www.sheltertheanimation.com/resources/img/pct_rin.png)，本机制作参考保存在 `assets/reference/rin-full.png`，官网原图不随公开源码分发。只参考图中左侧较大的Rin：粉棕色长发、白色发带、浅色短袖纽扣上衣和长侧摆、粉色短裤、蝴蝶结凉鞋。没有使用原对话的金发双马尾描述，也没有采用右侧幼年形象。

## 独立小跑图集

最终素材：`assets/rin/trot-v2.png`。使用内置 imagegen 工具、透明背景；首轮以现有 poses.png 为角色与服装参考、walk.png 为镜头参考。初版脚部跨入相邻格，未作为成品使用；针对留白与格内完整性进行一次修订。下面保留实际提示词。运行时根据头部与每行地面锚点对齐画面，避免裁切边距不同造成角色跳动。

### 首轮生成

```text
Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: production transparent 8-frame jog-cycle sprite atlas for the Windows desktop companion Rin’s Little Shelter. Input image 1 (poses.png) is the primary character identity, costume, proportions and painterly linework reference. Input image 2 (walk.png) is a supporting reference for the existing side/three-quarter camera angle and style, not an edit target. Generate a NEW distinct gentle jogging animation atlas of exactly the SAME Rin: muted pink-auburn long hair, white hair bow, light short-sleeved button tunic with side tails, dusty pink shorts, white ribbon sandals, about 4 heads tall, quiet warm expression. Preserve her face and clothing in all frames. Genuinely transparent alpha background. Exactly eight complete full-body frames arranged in a strict equal-cell 4-column by 2-row grid, read left to right across top row then bottom row, no lines, labels, numbers or text. All cells use one fixed camera, fixed character size, consistent head proportions, fixed body center, a shared ground baseline and enough transparent padding to keep hair, hands and sandals entirely within their cell. Character travels toward screen right, gentle three-quarter side view matching the reference, no scenery, no tablet or props. This MUST be a distinct coherent jogging cycle, not the existing walk sped up: slight forward body lean, softly bent elbows around the waist, alternating arm swing, visibly lifted alternating knees, heels folding behind, two subtle airborne phases. Frame 1 left foot landing in front and right leg folded behind; frame 2 left foot supports body, right knee swings forward; frame 3 gentle airborne phase left leg trails and right knee rises; frame 4 right foot extends for landing; frame 5 right foot lands and left heel folds; frame 6 right foot supports, left knee advances; frame 7 second gentle airborne phase with left knee raised; frame 8 left foot extends for landing into frame 1. Legs and arms change coherently and anatomically; head, body size and face stay stable. Keep the energy light and companion-like, small relaxed jog, no exaggerated sprint, no big grin, no aggressive bouncing, no duplicated poses. Preserve the reference soft clean anime illustration colors and edges. No shadows or opaque checkerboard background. The sheet is meant to be cropped into equal cells by game code, so exact 4x2 layout and complete unclipped figures are essential.
```

### 格内完整性修订

```text
Use case: identity-preserve. Production asset revision. Image 1 is the EDIT TARGET, an eight-frame gentle jog atlas of Rin. Image 2 is the PRIMARY IDENTITY AND STYLE REFERENCE. Preserve the EXACT same Rin face, pink-auburn long hair, white ribbon, pale short-sleeve tunic, pink shorts and white bow sandals and the eight distinct bent-arm jogging poses in reading order. Fix ONLY the atlas layout, padding and registration. The target currently has sandals from row 1 crossing into row 2. Create a transparent PNG with exactly 4 equal-width columns and 2 equal-height rows. Every frame must be entirely within its own cell, with very LARGE clear gutters above and below. Shrink each complete figure by at least 30% relative to its current cell size: figures should use only 60% of a cell's height and be centered vertically, leaving at least 20% transparent space above the head and 20% below the lowest sandal. Keep feet and hair at least 15% of cell width from both side boundaries. A VERY WIDE fully transparent horizontal gutter must separate rows. No part of the top row may extend into the bottom row. No part from one figure may appear near another figure's head. Maintain consistent head/body size and full character visibility. Preserve a genuine eight-frame coherent small jog cycle including alternating knees, bent elbows, and two subtle airborne phases. Same camera, same screen-right three-quarter direction, shared per-cell character center and contact-foot baseline. Soft calm expression, quiet companion energy. NO labels, numbers, borders, grid lines, props, scenery, floor, shadow, opaque backdrop, opaque checkerboard or additional objects. Real transparent alpha background. This layout will be cut into a STRICT uniform 4x2 grid by code; complete containment and large transparent gutters are more important than filling the canvas.
```

## 轻跃与边缘转身专用图集

最终素材：`assets/rin/hop.png`、`assets/rin/turn.png`，各12个独立姿势。使用内置 imagegen，透明背景；未使用CLI。输入 `poses.png` 是角色、脸型、服装与插画风格参考，`trot-v2.png` 是运动比例与镜头参考，两者都不是编辑目标。

图集按实测透明行间隙分格，避免直接等分裁掉鞋底；每帧的凉鞋锚点、站姿比例与裁切区域记录在 `assets/manifest.json`。蓄力和落地有真实屈膝、手臂与衣发变化；转身图集经过正面改变肩髋和鞋子角度。当前动作内部使用完整不透明姿势逐帧播放，起止过渡使用预乘RGBA线性合成，保持朝向和命中范围。曾额外尝试重新留白的布局修订，未选作最终素材；原图通过实际边缘检查后使用精确分格。

### 最终轻跃提示词（原文）

```text
Use case: illustration-story.
Asset type: production transparent sprite atlas for Rin's Little Shelter, a quiet Windows desktop companion.
Primary request: create ONE dedicated, finely animated 12-frame SMALL HOP sequence, not a translated standing picture. Input image 1 (poses.png) is the exact character identity and watercolor line style reference. Input image 2 (trot-v2.png) is a supporting movement/style reference only, not an edit target.
Character invariants: precisely the same gentle Rin, pink-brown long hair, white bow behind her hair, pale short-sleeved loose top with tiny pink details and long side hems, pink shorts, white ribbon sandals, same face, head size, limb length, approximately four-head semichibi proportion. Quiet, natural, no exaggerated expression, no accessories, no tablet.
Layout is essential: exactly 4 columns by 3 rows, 12 EQUAL rectangular cells, chronological left-to-right then top-to-bottom. Each cell contains exactly ONE complete isolated full-body Rin. Identical camera, scale, lighting. No character crosses a cell border; at least 30 pixels clear transparent gutter all around EVERY cell. No text, numbers, grid lines, background, platform, shadows, particles, floor or extra objects. True alpha transparency.
Animation: a gentle forward hop facing screen right, with three-quarter view so her face remains visible. Feet start and finish on the SAME ground baseline in every row. Use the SAME standing anatomical size even in compressed poses; do not resize a crouched character to fill its cell. Keep body horizontally centered at a consistent pelvis anchor, and all ground contact feet on a shared baseline at about 86% of cell height. A continuous sequence with real knee/ankle/hip/elbow joint changes:
Frame 1 relaxed standing, slight attentive glance ahead.
2 weight lowers slightly, knees soft, arms begin sweeping back.
3 deeper but modest crouch, hips lower, heels planted, hair lags upright.
4 push-off: knees extend, heels rise, toes still touch ground, arms swing forward.
5 takeoff: feet just airborne, knees beginning to tuck, arms relaxed forward.
6 rising: both knees bent, sandals lifted, hair trailing down and back.
7 apex: a SMALL hop only, knees softly tucked, arms balance, highest feet clearance roughly 10% cell height.
8 descending: knees start extending, sandals angled to contact, hair lifts gently from inertia.
9 forefeet touch shared ground line, knees still bend, torso upright.
10 weight settles into bent knees, heels grounded, arms lower, hair follows through.
11 knees extend to stand, hair settles gradually.
12 neutral settled stance like frame 1, tiny natural smile.
Maintain gradual motion arcs across neighboring frames, no sudden posture jumps. All clothing and hair drawn for each individual pose; real changed silhouettes. Match reference art exactly. Avoid squashing/stretching the whole character or simply rotating/translating one cutout. Production-ready clean transparent edges.
```

### 最终转身提示词（原文）

```text
Use case: illustration-story.
Asset type: production transparent sprite atlas for Rin's Little Shelter, a quiet Windows desktop companion.
Primary request: create ONE 12-frame delicate IN-PLACE HALF-TURN animation with actual head, shoulders, hips, stepping feet and flowing hair drawn from changing viewpoints. Input image 1 (poses.png) is the exact character identity, clothes and illustration style reference. Input image 2 (trot-v2.png) is a supporting proportions/style reference only, not an edit target.
Invariants: the SAME pink-brown long-haired Rin, white hair bow, pale short-sleeved patterned tunic with long side hems, pink shorts, white ribbon sandals. Approximately four-head semichibi, same face and anatomical proportions, same head size, quiet gentle expression. No props or tablet.
Layout: exactly 4 columns by 3 rows, 12 EQUAL cells, chronological left to right then top to bottom. Each cell contains ONE entire character with BOTH FEET and ALL HAIR fully inside its cell. Reduce the character enough to leave AT LEAST 45 PIXELS of completely transparent margin above and below EVERY cell and at least 35px left and right. Never overlap adjacent rows or columns. Feet share a ground baseline at 83% of EACH CELL'S height. Torso/pelvis centered on the same horizontal pivot. Keep exact same anatomical scale in every cell. Genuinely transparent background, no grid, no labels, no numbers, no shadows, no floor or objects.
Animation trajectory: start facing screen RIGHT in a gentle three-quarter front view, turn smoothly TOWARD CAMERA through front view, end facing screen LEFT in the corresponding three-quarter front view. This is one 180-degree direction change, NOT repeating a stationary front pose. No horizontal squash. Show actual shoulder widths and feet changing naturally by perspective, keeping bodily volume.
Frame 1: right-facing relaxed stance, both sandals on ground, arms relaxed.
2: eyes glance toward camera first, head begins looking over near shoulder, torso still right-facing.
3: neck turns more toward camera, shoulders begin following, near heel rises slightly, supporting sandal planted.
4: head nearly faces camera, shoulder angle opens, hips remain slightly right-facing, free foot begins small inward pivot.
5: shoulders near frontal, hips follow, a small step rotates the free sandal toward camera, long hair lags behind toward screen left.
6: fully FRONT-facing mid-turn, soft knees, weight transfers between feet; show complete front tunic, face and both sandals.
7: head looks toward screen left while torso is still nearly frontal, supporting foot pivots on ball, hair and bow follow a beat behind.
8: shoulders turn left, hips begin following, free foot takes a small short repositioning step.
9: left three-quarter stance, supporting heel lowers, arms counterbalance subtly, hair swings behind to screen right.
10: feet finish repositioning and are both grounded, head and torso now left-facing, hair slightly overshoots.
11: left-facing stance, hair and shirt hems settle with a small soft follow-through.
12: completely settled, relaxed left-facing three-quarter stance matching frame1 in volume and height, on identical ground baseline.
Neighboring frames should be subtle intermediate poses in a coherent hand-drawn animation; preserve identical face/wardrobe/lighting. Avoid frantic movement, cartoon spinning, motion lines, jump, wide steps, edited horizontal mirroring of a single pose, full-image rotate/scale cheats. Actual nuanced limb anatomy and head turns.
```
